# Source-document reconciliation for schematic capture

The workbook is the pin-by-pin connection source. Design Spec v1.1, Capture Update Note v1.0, and the detailed block/placement text in Schematic Design Notes v1.0 provide independent checks. The notes' Section 6 checklist retains a few earlier values that conflict with those current sources:

| Source location | Conflict | Capture used | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notes v1.0 Section 6, rule 4 | Calls C102 4.7 µF. | C102 2.2 µF, 25 V. | Spec v1.1 input/suspend text, Notes v1.0 Sections 3.1 and 3.5, Parts List v0.9, and the short-circuit/inrush calculations all use 2.2 µF. |
| Notes v1.0 Section 6, rule 16 | Places R227 on `MCLK`, after R203. | R227 connects `N2_X201_OUT` to GND, at X201 pin 3 before R203. | Notes v1.0 clock-block and placement text specify R227 at X201 pin 3; Parts List v0.9 matches. |
| Notes v1.0 Section 9, clock-routing table | One row calls the R665 MCLK-alive tap 1 kΩ. | R665 330 Ω. | Notes v1.0 Sections 3.2/3.6/9.2, Spec v1.1, and Parts List v0.9 consistently use 330 Ω. |
| Notes v1.0 Section 6, rule 44 | Traces the series permit only through Q616 and Q617. | Q622 follows Q617 in the permit path. | Notes v1.0 rule 58 and the detailed re-arm-latch section, Spec v1.1, and Parts List v0.9 all include Q622. |
| Parts List v0.9, C442/C443 Datasheet field | Names Kyocera AVX TAJD227K010RNJ but links a Kemet T495 page. | The KiCad property links the [KYOCERA AVX TAJ datasheet](https://datasheets.kyocera-avx.com/TAJ.pdf). | The specified MPN is a TAJ-series capacitor; its manufacturer's sheet identifies the polarity band as the anode (+). The JLC C8024 footprint band is at pad 1, matching the workbook's pin-1-positive map. |
| Parts List v0.9, Netlist `A340` | During capture the cell read `G1`, creating a nonexistent designator and omitting D705 pad 1. | The owner approved correcting that cell to `D705` on 27 September 2026. | The Parts list, Capture Update Note v1.0, and Calculation Package v1.1 all identify this row as D705 pad 1. The repaired workbook matches the package's 1,445 pin rows. |
| Spec v1.1 / Parts List v0.9 slot-ring open point | Maker copper leaves J701 at 0.25 mm nominal / 0.20 mm at the source's +0.10 mm slot tolerance and J702 at 0.20 mm, below the design's 0.30 mm rule. | On 27 September 2026 the owner chose copper-only enlargement. The JLC tolerance check uses +0.13 mm, giving J701 pads 2.15 × 1.25 mm and J702 slot pads 2.25 × 1.35 mm. | Slot drills, pad centres and locating holes stay fixed. The footprints now reach ≥0.31 mm worst ring; the closest J701 pad gap is 0.25 mm. G-3 overlay and JLCPCB DFM still decide physical acceptance. The versioned source documents predate this owner decision. |
| Spec v1.1 G-1/G-2 signed sample logs | The versioned release protocol requires identified samples, raw continuity readings and signed tables before routing. | After reporting two-sample connector checks, the owner explicitly waived the G-1/G-2 measurement log on 27 September 2026. The reported maps are owner-accepted for layout planning without independent evidence. | `PRELAYOUT_GATES.md` preserves the actual reported checks and the waiver. G-3 overlay and G-4 polarity still hold routing; any later contact-map discrepancy requires correction. |
| Parts List v0.9 / Spec v1.1 U403–U404 | Specify OPA2210IDR in SOIC-8 and DAC-to-I/V traces ≤5 mm. | The owner approved OPA2210IDGKR in VSSOP-8, JLCPCB C2876414, and selected reassessment of a provisional ≤7 mm trace rule after the SOIC package could not meet 5 mm. | TI's D/DGK pin table is identical. `generate_schematic.py` asserts and overrides the old workbook MPN/package without changing any pin/net entry. `IV_7MM_RECHECK.md` records the 1,080-corner stability sensitivity, placement probe, JLC package reuse, and remaining route/extraction and sourcing gates. The source DOCX/XLSX files still need a revision before manufacturing freeze. |
| Calculation Package v1.1 I/V gain-margin headline | Summarizes “GM ≥5.6 dB” alongside the lowest phase margin. | The recheck finds 5.6 dB at the lowest-phase-margin corner, but a 4.46 dB global minimum gain margin at another model corner in the original 2–5 pF sweep. | `analog/scripts/stability_iv.py` reports only the gain margin of its minimum-PM corner. `IV_7MM_RECHECK.md` separates the two extrema and gives the 6 pF sensitivity result. |
| Spec v1.1 / Notes v1.0 board outline and J701 ESD target | The source placement uses 100 × 80 mm and the ESD placement target is ≤3 mm. | On 27 September 2026 the owner first selected 100 × 100 mm, then allowed the **120 × 100 mm** manual primary. The owner also approved a **provisional ≤4.2 mm routed J701 TVS signal limit**, short L2 GND returns and a system IEC ESD test. J703 stays schematic DNF with no PCB pads. | The four routed TVS escapes are 3.83–4.03 mm; the remaining board is unrouted. `MANUAL_PLACEMENT_REVIEW.md` records the routability and EMI holds. The source DOCX/XLSX documents need controlled revisions before manufacturing freeze. |
| Functional review F01 / source all-rate guard | Source documents credit an MCU I²S2 post-CPLD capture/guard through 384 kHz. WCH's current CH32V307 v3.9 datasheet still limits I²S CK to 8 MHz, exceeded from 176.4 kHz up. | The [SPI2 slave/DMA capture contract](MCU_SPI2_CAPTURE_ECO.md) uses the same PB13/PB15 pins with a published 72 MHz SPI limit; no pin/net change is made. | RTL/firmware, DMA arbitration, deterministic framing and physical WS-path fault coverage are not implemented or proven. F01 and the all-rate R-28 energy credit remain **open**. RME's on-demand Bit Test example does not justify a 96 kHz guard cutoff for this circuit. |
| Functional review F02 / R688–R689 | The v0.9 direct 470 kΩ readbacks may violate U201 FT-input VIH/VIL at ±3 µA leakage. Simply lowering them can weaken Q619/Q620 gate isolation. | The [asserted ECO](FUNCTIONAL_ECO_2026-09-28.md) keeps 470 kΩ isolation and adds U621 dual Schmitt buffer, R952/R953 output series, R954/R955 fail-high pulls and C667 bypass. | Conservative level calculations pass, but exact rail/temperature response, comparator hysteresis, fault-state timing and the U621 JLC/TI land difference require tests/G-3. The old workbook/package remain the baseline. |
| Functional review F03 / 3V3M attach charge | Notes v1.0 says 5.1 µF, while Calculation Package v1.1 c22 assumes 6.05 µF; the fitted baseline is 6.4 µF. | The new C667 makes the captured nominal sum 6.5 µF. [Inventory-derived screen](ATTACH_CHARGE_RECONCILIATION.json) gives 43.695 µC Region A without the former load credit under its stated C(V,T) assumption. | Measure capacitance, region overlap/inrush and U502 output-cap stability before USB qualification; update the controlled calculation package. |
| Functional review F04 / J702 ESD | D701/D702 at J701 are tens of millimetres from the shared LP/RP J702 contacts. | The [asserted ECO](FUNCTIONAL_ECO_2026-09-28.md) adds D707/D708 locally; their trial jack branches measure 3.861/3.250 mm and return to nearby L2 vias. | Complete the audio trunks, nonlinear loading/THD and IEC system ESD review; the source Office set predates this part addition. |

`verify_design_notes.py` checks 978 selected machine-checkable pin, value, net-membership, assembly-status and approved-correction assertions. It does not cover firmware, PCB placement/routing, thermal design, or physical gates. The J701/J702 maker maps, owner-accepted connector tests, approved LED correction and still-open G-3/G-4 work are recorded in `CONNECTOR_REVIEW.md`, `POLARITY_REVIEW.md` and `PRELAYOUT_GATES.md`.
