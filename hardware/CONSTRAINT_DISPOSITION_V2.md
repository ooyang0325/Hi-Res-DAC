# Routing and fabrication constraints for the integrated-audio study

**Status:** engineering review policy for the 120 × 100 mm candidate. The [DAC/core checkpoint](DAC_CORE_ROUTE_STUDY.md) and [audit](DAC_CORE_ROUTE_AUDIT.json) supersede this document's earlier DAC/CPLD route measurements. This records which rules are implemented now and which need routed or measured evidence; it does not release manufacturing data. The four-layer stack keeps one continuous L2 GND plane; analog/digital separation is by placement and controlled current paths, with no ground-plane split.

## KiCad and JLCPCB geometry reconciliation

`DAC_HPA.kicad_pro` now uses a Ø0.60/0.20 mm default signal via and a Ø0.80/0.40 mm power preset; both give a 0.20 mm nominal ring. A separate `FINE_ESCAPE` class offers 0.15 mm width/clearance when deliberately assigned during fanout. No 0.10 mm track is approved by the current project floor. The integrated board's U501 courtyard rule permits only its three short 0.20 mm VIN escapes below the usual 0.8 mm `5V_ANA_F` width; the checker bounds each branch. The jack's elongated plated-slot ring target ≥0.30 mm is a **different** mechanical land rule and is not imposed on every signal via. [Routing-policy check](verify_routing_policy.py) verifies the settings and actual USB/power/audio net assignments.

The published [JLCPCB multilayer capability limits](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/Capab) include 0.09/0.09 mm trace/space for 1 oz and a 0.15 mm absolute / 0.20 mm recommended PTH ring. The project retains 0.15 mm track/clearance and 0.20 mm via-ring design floors. The [JLC impedance calculator](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/user-guide-to-the-jlcpcb-impedance-calculator) requires the quoted stack-up and spacing; no one width guarantees 90 Ω for every order. [JLC assembly terms](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/terms-and-conditions-of-jlcpcb-assembly-service) and its package-spacing table still require an order-specific review, particularly for edge connectors and plated slots.

At 50 °C, the calculation package uses 0.551 mΩ per square for 35 µm copper. Thus 20 mm × 0.5 mm and 60 mm × 1.5 mm are both about 22.0 mΩ, while 50 mm × 1.0 mm is 27.5 mΩ. A 1 mm × 0.25 mm VSON escape is about 2.20 mΩ, approximately 1.10 mΩ more than a 1 mm × 0.5 mm segment. These are **DC resistance comparisons only**. A standalone 40 mm × 3 mm strip is about 7.35 mΩ before vias, beyond the simple 5.7 mΩ common-return ceiling; the real L2 plane contributes parallel paths and requires extraction/measurement.

The source Spec/Notes and older reviews still state some superseded 20 mm/3 mm/1 mm numerical instructions. This table is the routing-study disposition; their controlled document revision remains open before manufacturing freeze. Physical G-3/G-4 and JLC process gates do not change.
