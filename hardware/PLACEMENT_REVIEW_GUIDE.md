# Placement, routability and PCBA peer-review guide

Start with the 120 × 100 mm integrated-audio study and its [electrical-margin review](INTEGRATED_AUDIO_ROUTE_STUDY.md), then use the newer [DAC/core route checkpoint](DAC_CORE_ROUTE_STUDY.md) for the current DAC/CPLD route measurements. Compare with the [functional-ECO capture baseline](DAC_HPA_120x100_FUNCTIONAL_ECO_STUDY_ONLY.kicad_pcb), its [whole-board preview](DAC_HPA_FUNCTIONAL_ECO_PLACEMENT_REVIEW.png) and [layout review](FUNCTIONAL_ECO_LAYOUT_REVIEW.md). The GitLab `deliver_review_package` artifact provides zoomable SVG and GLB 3D views for the reviewed commit. Record board coordinates, layer and references for findings. The earlier 536-footprint primary and 100 × 80/100 × 100 mm boards do not match the eight-part ECO schematic.

The [earlier macro-placement review](MACRO_PLACEMENT_REVIEW.md) explains the first subcircuit regrouping. The functional-ECO baseline improves the DAC supply-capacitor row and hold-up-reservoir access, and adds the J702 local TVS/readback parts. The [intermediate 270° output study](OUTPUT_MACRO_ROUTE_STUDY.md) proves four amplifier-to-relay inputs and feedback loops. The integrated study continues those paths to the physical jack contacts and adds DAC/I/V-to-T and U501 local copper under the [A/B-channel schematic ECO](FUNCTIONAL_ECO_2026-09-28.md). Use the [constraint disposition](CONSTRAINT_DISPOSITION_V2.md) for each board.

For scalable per-layer views, run the one-command [CLI visual inspection workflow](PCB_CLI_VISUAL_REVIEW.md) on the integrated board without modifying it.

**Board and route snapshots:** The functional-ECO capture baseline has 544 footprints, 250 named PCB nets, 28 F.Cu tracks, seven vias and one filled L2 GND zone; it contains J701/J702 TVS branches and the 8.311 mm X201→R203→U301 MCLK trunk. The later integrated-audio candidate has 544 footprints, 250 named nets and 855 track/via items (143 vias), with **zero KiCad DRC violations, 499 DRC-reported unconnected items and 864 full ratsnest links**. Its current manual record is **138 explicit moves, eight removed source copper items and 828 added copper items**, zero exact 90° and free 80–100° track bends, zero classified JLC spacing findings, four DAC→I/V routes, eight I/V→first-T feeds, eight connected local T triplets, U501 local switching/feedback copper and four connected amplifier-to-jack output paths. The newer [DAC/core checkpoint](DAC_CORE_ROUTE_STUDY.md) measures MCLK at **8.381 mm**, BCLK/LRCLK/SDATA at **22.213/22.324/22.879 mm**, and the connected U303→DAC 1V3 route at **25.393 mm** with two vias. Two U202 and five U301 EP vias are filled/capped; the via-pad audit reports 45 nearby unfilled sites for JLC mask/process review. U401/U402 output-amplifier EP thermal paths remain open. Full routing, system ESD, EMI, audio testing and JLC process acceptance remain open. All 478 populated component footprints resolve 3D bodies.

## Review order

| Priority | What to establish | Present evidence and open test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Connector and hand access | Physical jack/USB fit, board-edge mating, top-side soldering access and package-to-package space. | [G-3 1:1 overlays](G3_OVERLAY_INDEX.md) and [G-4 polarity](G4_POLARITY_CHECKLIST.md) are open. J101 and J702 need JLCPCB acceptance at finished edges and a confirmed soldering process for plated slots. J701's 1.5 mm top-side iron access is preserved in the manual primary. |
| 2. Critical simultaneous routes | Establish drawable paths with feedback and returns present. | See the route-risk table below. DAC/I/V signal copper is drawn, but long right-side cross-layer feeds, true feedback/return loops, amplifier source rails and U501 return currents still need extraction and validation. The board remains partly unrouted. |
| 3. Audio and EMI topology | Preserve a single continuous L2 GND reference, quiet I/V/VREF, isolated high-impedance nodes, and a dedicated jack return. | The present L2 GND zone covers the board, but no complete output/clock/power return-current review is possible until routes, stitching and L4 pours exist. Use [Audio/EMI placement review](AUDIO_EMI_PLACEMENT_REVIEW.md), reading its old 100 × 100 mm examples as historical. |
| 4. Fabrication and assembly | Check actual JLCPCB policy, design rules, custom lands, pick-and-place access and BOM fit. | [JLC DFA/DFM rules](JLCPCB_DFA_DFM_RULES.md) and audits are screens only. The current conservative proxy classifies the new U621 and SOD-523 devices; 16 placed references remain outside its package table. Enlarged jack lands, panel rails and slot soldering need order-specific review. |

The manual primary removed the historical 15 sensitive-to-clock *pad* gap findings, but routed copper must still satisfy the 5/10 mm high-impedance separation rules. The integrated board's custom DRC also applies **2 mm F.Cu left/right clearance before the 0 Ω links**: `N4_*_OUT` tracks are checked against the opposite channel's pre-link and `LEG_*`/`JACK_*` copper. The existing 2 mm post-link rule remains. The integrated checker rejects exact 90° and free-copper 80–100° bends; pad exits and true T/cross joins are classified separately. J701's fixed contact pitch needs a narrow local exception for tracks crossing its courtyard against J701 pads, at ≥0.20 mm clearance. Inspect that fanout and the L4 output trunks for crosstalk and manufacturability.

## DFA/DFM and physical checks

- Overlay actual J101/J701/J702, relay, oscillator, U621, ESD and polarized parts on the [30-pattern G-3 sheets](G3_OVERLAY_INDEX.md); verify terminal overlap, pin 1, body/peg/slot fit and the enlarged jack copper. J701/J702 worst calculated slot ring is ≥0.31 mm under JLC's published +0.13 mm slot-size tolerance; slot-to-copper registration and the 0.25 mm nearest J701 pad gap still require JLC DFM.
- Inspect C423/C426's **0.150–0.194 mm** courtyard gaps, the tight C437/J701/K602 input corridor and small-capacitor via mask/tenting in the JLC order DFM. The zero-violation proxy does not prove solder mask or assembly access there.
- Inspect J101 and J702 mating edges and panel rails with JLCPCB. The current proxy flags them as assembly exceptions to the general 2.5 mm body-to-finished-edge screen. J101's 3D shell was aligned to the existing maker PCB-edge datum; solder lands did not move.
- Confirm soldering of J101 S1–S4 and J702 1/2 plated slots. They currently have no F.Paste apertures. Check the proposed bottom-side J702 hand-solder fallback and access before changing fit class. [PCBA process review](PCBA_PROCESS_REVIEW.md) contains the exact JLC question.
- Check owner-fitted relay and film-capacitor iron access, 3D heights and thermal exposure. The C631–C634 body is a review envelope, and X201–X203 use a generic 2520 model; use physical parts and maker drawings at G-3.
- Review the current `INTEGRATED_AUDIO_DFA_AUDIT.json` in CI: zero classified spacing/edge findings and 16 unclassified placed references. Zero proxy findings do not guarantee machine access or final soldering. Confirm BOM/rotation in JLC's order preview after layout.

## Reproduce the read-only checks

Use KiCad 10's Python interpreter with `pcbnew` (`python3` in the GitLab KiCad image; on macOS the bundled interpreter path is shown in [README](README.md)). From the repository root:

```sh
board=hardware/DAC_HPA_120x100_INTEGRATED_AUDIO_STUDY_ONLY.kicad_pcb
python3 hardware/audit_placement.py "$board" --height 100 > /tmp/placement.json
python3 hardware/audit_dfa_dfm.py "$board" > /tmp/dfa_dfm.json
python3 hardware/audit_jlc_fab.py "$board" > /tmp/jlc_fab.json
kicad-cli pcb drc --format json --severity-error --severity-warning -o /tmp/pcb_drc.json "$board"
python3 hardware/audit_local_tvs_paths.py "$board" --output /tmp/local_tvs.json
python3 hardware/audit_audio_output_traces.py "$board" --output /tmp/trace_budget.json
python3 hardware/check_integrated_audio_study.py "$board" /tmp/placement.json /tmp/dfa_dfm.json /tmp/jlc_fab.json /tmp/pcb_drc.json /tmp/local_tvs.json /tmp/trace_budget.json --output /tmp/integrated_summary.json
python3 hardware/audit_3d_models.py "$board" --output /tmp/model_3d.json
```

Use [Review findings template](REVIEW_FINDINGS_TEMPLATE.md) to separate **observed board defects** from **route feasibility concerns** and **physical/JLC answers still needed**. A passing script is evidence for its stated screen only. Routing release remains on hold until G-3/G-4 and the listed geometric and audio/EMI checks are closed.
