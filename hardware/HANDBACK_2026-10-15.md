## 3. What changed in `hardware/cpld/`

| File | Change | Why |

| `U202_pins.asf` | CPLD_NRST on `PIN_NRST` instead of `PIN_4` | Pin 4 is the dedicated NRST pad; Supra has no `PIN_4` |

## 4. AGRV2K datasheet (Rev 3.0) findings

- **NRST (pin 4).** Confirmed as the NRST pin. The datasheet does not say what NRST does: power-on reset
  only watches VDD33 and loads the configuration. **Open:** does NRST also reload the configuration? Ask
  AGM, or check at bring-up. Either way the RTL reset is harmless.
- **4 global clock lines, all in use** by the fit: LINK_SCK, FAM_CLK, CPLD_NRST, the frame clear. The
  product RTL has none spare. If it needs one, the two clears can move to ordinary routing.
- **4 M9K blocks, all in use** by the 1024 × 32 FIFO.
- **Drive strength.** Programmable, but no mA values are given. Slow slew stays the R2 measure.
- **Marking.** "AGRV2K" is AGM's software name for the CPLD in the AG32 MCU. Parts arrive marked
  `AG32VF?03K?U6`, not `AGRV2KQ32`. LCSC C18208899 is listed as `AGRV2KQ32`, so the BOM line is right.
  Expect the AG32VF marking at G-3/G-4 and in JLC's placement preview.
