# Schematic peer-review guide

Review the committed [root schematic](DAC_HPA.kicad_sch) and eight linked sheets in KiCad 10, or start with the [nine-page PDF](DAC_HPA_review_only.pdf). The root is PDF page 1; circuit sheets 1–8 are PDF pages 2–9. Record the Git SHA and sheet/page in every finding.

## Source and automated-check boundary

The v0.9 workbook supplies 1,445 baseline pin rows across 247 workbook nets including NC. The [asserted ECO](FUNCTIONAL_ECO_2026-09-28.md) produces a capture with 1,465 pins, 1,422 connected pins, 43 no-connect pins and 250 named nets. `verify_schematic.py` compares the exact 34 pin/net deltas, MPN/fit fields and footprint pads with that baseline; `verify_design_notes.py` checks 1,002 selected rules, and ERC reports zero violations. These are **consistency checks**. Inspect datasheet pin function, power limits, startup/fault behavior, analog stability and physical pad mapping independently.

[Source reconciliation](SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md) and the [functional ECO](FUNCTIONAL_ECO_2026-09-28.md) list controlled deviations from the versioned Office set. A reviewer should test the reason for each exception. Do not silently replace the approved OPA2210 VSSOP or its explicit A/B channel assignment, corrected LED polarity, connector maps, U621 readback buffer or D707/D708 with an older source value. `generate_schematic.py` rewrites generated schematic files; use a clean checkout if testing regeneration.

## Cross-sheet checks to return explicitly

1. **End-to-end path:** USB attach and bridge enable → stable clocks/I²S → DAC and analog rails → output permit/relay → both jacks. Identify any state where an output can connect before rails and offsets are safe.
2. **Protection:** for over-range, low-level persistence, rail loss, hot-plug, short and self-test, trace both channels from detector to muted output. Challenge single-fault and stuck-high/low assumptions using the Spec and Calculation Package. The [review-only R417–R420 output-link candidate](OUTPUT_LINK_BOM_ECO.md) replaces the v1.1 model's 50 mΩ per link with a 1 mΩ manufacturer maximum. The integrated board's conditional left 4.4 mm model is 0.3862 Ω at 1 kHz and 0.3990 Ω at 20 kHz; the latter assumes the same ≤0.5 Ω limit applies there. The 50 mΩ per jack-contact estimate, 3.5 mm sleeve return and loaded behavior remain unverified. Measure both jacks over the intended band before closing R-15.
3. **Source corrections:** verify C102 2.2 µF, R227 at `N2_X201_OUT`, R665 330 Ω, Q622 in permit, U403/U404 DGK VSSOP and their A/B channel swap, and D705/D706 polarity against [Source reconciliation](SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md).
4. **Physical mapping:** compare symbol pin numbers to footprint pads for J101/J701/J702, U202 exposed pad, polarized parts, oscillators and owner-fitted relays. Use [G-3 overlays](G3_OVERLAY_INDEX.md) and [G-4 record](G4_POLARITY_CHECKLIST.md). G-1/G-2 are owner-accepted with raw-log waiver; this waiver does not extend to G-3/G-4.
5. **Assembly split:** check the placed/owner-fitted/DNF classification in the three review CSVs. The 463 JLC-placed references and 14 references without LCSC codes are a review snapshot, not an order-ready BOM. Verify the process holds in [PCBA process review](PCBA_PROCESS_REVIEW.md).

For each checked path, report **pass**, **issue**, **needs measurement** or **not reviewed** in the [findings template](REVIEW_FINDINGS_TEMPLATE.md). Include the governing datasheet/table or measured waveform when proposing a change; a clean ERC result alone is not sufficient evidence.
