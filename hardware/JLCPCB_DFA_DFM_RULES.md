# JLCPCB fabrication and assembly rules for the DAC-HPA review boards

Checked against JLCPCB's published [PCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities), [SMD package spacing table](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/minimum-spacing-for-smd-components), and [PCBA assembly terms](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/terms-and-conditions-of-jlcpcb-assembly-service) on 27 September 2026. These are source-backed design screens; the order-specific Gerber/drill/CPL review by JLCPCB is still required.

| JLCPCB published limit or condition | KiCad review setting / status |
| --- | --- |
| Multilayer 1 oz minimum track/space 0.09/0.09 mm; multilayer 2 oz 0.15/0.15 mm (the 2-layer 2 oz figure is 0.16/0.16 mm) | Project minima 0.15/0.15 mm; USB pair uses 0.235 mm nominal width with short 0.15 mm neck-downs. Copper weight and controlled-impedance stackup remain for the JLC quote. |
| Multilayer 1 oz PTH ring absolute 0.15 mm, recommended 0.20 mm | Project via-ring minimum 0.20 mm. The intended via geometry is Ø0.60/0.20 mm, pending routing and drill review. |
| Preferred via drill ≥0.20 mm; multilayer absolute hole/pad 0.15/0.25 mm | Project minimum through-hole diameter 0.20 mm and via diameter 0.60 mm. The four routed TVS GND vias are through vias at Ø0.60/0.20 mm with 0.20 mm ring, checked by `audit_jlc_fab.py`. Blind/buried vias are not used. |
| PTH-hole to track ≥0.28 mm | Project hole-clearance minimum 0.28 mm. |
| Copper to routed edge ≥0.20 mm; copper to V-cut edge ≥0.40 mm | Project copper-edge clearance 0.50 mm. Deliberate connector overhangs are mechanical outlines, not copper-edge exceptions. |
| Silk line ≥0.15 mm, text height ≥1.0 mm, pad-to-silk ≥0.15 mm | Project silkscreen clearance, text and thickness minima match those values. |
| SMD pad-to-pad clearance ≥0.15 mm; 1 oz solder-mask bridge ≥0.10 mm for green/red/yellow/blue/purple, ≥0.13 mm for black/white; 2 oz bridge ≥0.20 mm | Project clearance is 0.15 mm. J701's enlarged adjacent pads leave 0.25 mm copper and nominal mask gap with 1:1 openings. Final mask color, expansion and fabrication registration need JLC DFM review. |
| Multilayer plated-slot width table: ≥0.35 mm; the same page's explanatory text says ≥0.50 mm | All J101/J701/J702 plated slots are ≥0.50 mm wide; slot lengths are at least twice the width. |
| Plated-slot size tolerance +0.13/−0.08 mm | `audit_jlc_fab.py` applies +0.13 mm to both slot axes. J101 shell stakes now have 0.205 mm worst ring; J701 and J702 have 0.310 mm. All copper changes preserve maker slot centres and drills and remain subject to G-3 sample overlay. |
| Component body to finished board edge ≥2.5 mm for PCBA | The classified JLC-placed parts pass the body proxy. J101 overhangs the left edge by 0.05 mm and J702 sits only 0.33 mm from the right edge. Both are explicit **JLC assembly exceptions pending acceptance of the connector and panel-rail strategy**. The planned 5 mm rails alone do not establish acceptance at these two mating edges. |
| Package-pair minimums vary by package, e.g. 0402/0402 0.15 mm and 0402/QFN 1.0 mm | `audit_dfa_dfm.py` applies JLC's table to 435 classified JLC-placed references using F.Fab body bounds. The latest hand placements have zero findings in this proxy. Twenty JLC-placed references have packages outside the table, including the jacks, TVS diodes, oscillators and film capacitors; these require individual review. |

The KiCad `.kicad_pro` files set the global fabrication minima. `.kicad_dru` adds the electrical and assembly rules specific to this audio design: solid GND L2, L1 signal restrictions, 0.5 mm headphone outputs, 2 mm L/R separation outside the maker's J701 contact fanout, 1.5 mm owner-fit access, timing and clock path lengths, and 5/10 mm sensitive-net separation from clocks. The owner-approved provisional 4.2 mm J701 TVS signal route and its ESD test are also **design requirements, not JLCPCB policy**. KiCad's ordinary DRC does not apply JLC's package-pair table or finished-body edge rule; the read-only audit provides that screen.

The G-3 1:1 overlays and JLC's actual fabrication review must confirm the enlarged slot copper, the 0.25 mm closest J701 pad-to-pad copper gap, mask openings, pin-in-paste or hand-solder process, mechanical pegs, and the edge-connector panel arrangement. The revised J701 pad gap remains above the 0.15 mm project clearance and JLC multilayer 1 oz floor, but fabrication registration is not yet checked by an order upload.
