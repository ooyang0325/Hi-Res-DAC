# JLCPCB fabrication and assembly rules for the DAC-HPA review boards

Checked against JLCPCB's published [PCB capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/Capab), [SMD package spacing table](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/minimum-spacing-for-smd-components), and [PCBA assembly terms](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/terms-and-conditions-of-jlcpcb-assembly-service) on 28 September 2026. These are source-backed design screens; the order-specific Gerber/drill/CPL review by JLCPCB is still required.

| JLCPCB published limit or condition | KiCad review setting / status |
| --- | --- |
| Multilayer 1 oz minimum track/space 0.09/0.09 mm; multilayer 2 oz 0.15/0.15 mm (the 2-layer 2 oz figure is 0.16/0.16 mm) | Project minima 0.15/0.15 mm. The new `USB_DIFF` netclass assigns 0.235 mm nominal width, 0.15 mm gap and 0.15 mm clearance; a real 90 Ω result awaits the quoted JLC stack-up and field solver. |
| Multilayer 1 oz PTH ring absolute 0.15 mm, recommended 0.20 mm | Project via-ring minimum 0.20 mm. The default signal preset is now Ø0.60/0.20 mm; a Ø0.80/0.40 mm power preset also gives a 0.20 mm ring. |
| Preferred via drill ≥0.20 mm; multilayer absolute hole/pad 0.15/0.25 mm | Project minimum through-hole diameter 0.20 mm and via diameter 0.60 mm. The four routed TVS GND vias are through vias at Ø0.60/0.20 mm with 0.20 mm ring, checked by `audit_jlc_fab.py`. Blind/buried vias are not used. |
| PTH-hole to track ≥0.28 mm | Project hole-clearance minimum 0.28 mm. |
| Copper to routed edge ≥0.20 mm; copper to V-cut edge ≥0.40 mm | Project copper-edge clearance 0.50 mm. Deliberate connector overhangs are mechanical outlines, not copper-edge exceptions. |
| Silk line ≥0.15 mm, text height ≥1.0 mm, pad-to-silk ≥0.15 mm | Project silkscreen clearance, text and thickness minima match those values. |
| SMD pad-to-pad clearance ≥0.15 mm; 1 oz solder-mask bridge ≥0.10 mm for green/red/yellow/blue/purple, ≥0.13 mm for black/white; 2 oz bridge ≥0.20 mm | Project clearance is 0.15 mm. Both are explicit **JLC assembly exceptions pending acceptance of the connector and panel-rail strategy**. The planned 5 mm rails alone do not establish acceptance at these two mating edges. |
| Package-pair minimums vary by package, e.g. 0402/0402 0.15 mm and 0402/QFN 1.0 mm | `audit_dfa_dfm.py` applies JLC's table to 447 classified references out of 463 JLC-placed references, using F.Fab drawing rectangles where available and footprint bounds otherwise. The [current integrated-audio audit](INTEGRATED_AUDIO_DFA_AUDIT.json) reports zero package-pair and classified body-edge proxy findings. Sixteen JLC-placed references have packages outside the table, including connectors, TVS diodes, oscillators and film capacitors; these require individual review. |

The latest integrated layout check records zero footprint bounding-box overlaps and zero classified JLC spacing findings. These are geometry screens, not order acceptance; 16 references remain outside the package-spacing table, and connector, film-capacitor and via-mask processes still need review. The current board has **132 vias**. The via-pad audit flags **45 unfilled via-to-SMT-pad sites within 0.35 mm** for JLC mask/process review, with zero unfilled copper overlaps and zero unfilled gaps below 0.10 mm.

The U610 timer placement uses a pair-specific **0.60 mm side-courtyard exception** for C633/C634 film capacitors while preserving at least **1.5 mm north/south solder approach**, checked by the [film solder-access audit](INTEGRATED_AUDIO_FILM_SOLDER_ACCESS_AUDIT.json). This is a project-specific allowance, not a published JLC package-pair minimum. Verify the actual capacitor bodies and solder process in G-3 and the order preview. U610's six named-pad timer branches meet the ≤8 mm project limit with no timer vias; C638's two-via L3 VNEG bypass bridge also needs review with the full board return design.

The primary KiCad `.kicad_pro` sets global minima and separate USB, power, audio-output and reserved fine-escape netclasses. `verify_routing_policy.py` checks their assignments and ensures every via preset has a ≥0.20 mm nominal ring. The unassigned `FINE_ESCAPE` class is for reviewed local 0.15 mm fanout; the older Notes' 0.10 mm suggestion is not enabled. The integrated board's `.kicad_dru` keeps solid GND L2, L1 signal restrictions, a 0.25 mm headphone-output width floor for the reviewed 0.932 mm VSON pad escapes, 2 mm L/R separation outside the maker's J701 contact fanout, 1.5 mm owner-fit access, timing/clock path limits and 5/10 mm sensitive-net separation from clocks. The current study widens those local escapes to ≥0.50 mm under the project [routing constraint disposition](CONSTRAINT_DISPOSITION_V2.md); this is not a JLCPCB package rule. The owner-approved provisional 4.2 mm J701 TVS signal route and its ESD test are **design requirements, not JLCPCB policy**. That disposition also covers the 20 mm audio, 3 mm return-strip, bypass and timer-rule interpretation. KiCad's ordinary DRC does not apply JLC's package-pair table or finished-body edge rule; the read-only audit provides that screen.

The G-3 1:1 overlays and JLC's actual fabrication review must confirm the enlarged slot copper, the 0.25 mm closest J701 pad-to-pad copper gap, mask openings, pin-in-paste or hand-solder process, mechanical pegs, and the edge-connector panel arrangement. The revised J701 pad gap remains above the 0.15 mm project clearance and JLC multilayer 1 oz floor, but fabrication registration is not yet checked by an order upload.
