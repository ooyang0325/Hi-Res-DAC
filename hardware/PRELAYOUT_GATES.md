## Decisions already in force

- The owner allowed a 120 × 100 mm primary, four-layer manual placement after the 100 × 100 mm comparison. The 100 × 80 mm and 100 × 100 mm automatic boards remain review comparisons. Top-side Standard PCBA and the owner-fitted list remain as in Spec v1.1. The source document's outline needs revision before manufacturing freeze.
- U403/U404 use the owner-approved OPA2210IDGKR VSSOP-8, JLCPCB C2876414, with positive DAC legs on physical B and negative legs on A. DACL/DACLB/DACR/DACRB routed lengths are **4.294/6.030/6.925/4.786 mm** on F.Cu without vias, under the provisional ≤7 mm target; DACR has only **0.075 mm** margin. Saved L2 GND is directly beneath all four inputs at 0.01 mm route samples and offsets 0, ±0.05, ±0.10 mm. The source documents still specify SOIC-8, the earlier A/B assignment and ≤5 mm; extracted capacitance, 3D feedback/return geometry and step response must close the reassessment.
- The 3.5 mm output is for TRS plugs only. The HRO jack has a TRRS body, but TRRS headphones are outside the supported use; G-2 needs the intended TRS test, which the owner reports matched on both samples. The PCB silkscreen/user instructions should say “3.5 SE · TRS only”.
- The v1.1 defaults remain adopted: pairwise boot self-test with production per-leg injection, the Spec's DC-energy definition, and the High-mode 32 Ω frozen-full-scale engineering estimate with a bring-up energy test. Reopening self-test coverage would add parts and must happen before routing.
- The v1.1 model assumed 50 mΩ per jack contact and per 0 Ω output link. The [review-only R417–R420 BOM candidate](OUTPUT_LINK_BOM_ECO.md) has a 1 mΩ manufacturer maximum per link; the unchanged contact estimate has no maker maximum. With that candidate, the integrated board's conditional worst 4.4 mm estimates are **0.3862/0.3737 Ω (L/R) at 1 kHz** and **0.3990/0.3864 Ω at 20 kHz**. The latter is a model sensitivity if the same ≤0.5 Ω limit applies there. Measure both jacks over the intended audio band, extract the 3.5 mm sleeve return and confirm R-15's frequency scope; this is not a pre-layout measurement.
- The owner chose at least a 0.30 mm worst copper ring by enlarging copper only. Using JLCPCB's +0.13 mm plated-slot tolerance, J701/J702 now reach ≥0.31 mm worst ring. Slots, pad centres and peg holes do not move. G-3 overlay and JLCPCB DFM acceptance remain required.
- The ring calculation does not include slot-to-copper registration error; JLCPCB must confirm the final land in DFM. The nearest J701 pad-to-pad copper gap is 0.25 mm.
- The integrated study's custom DRC enforces **2 mm F.Cu left/right clearance for pre-link `N4_*_OUT` copper** against the opposite channel's output/leg/jack copper, in addition to the existing post-link 2 mm rule. The J701 fixed-contact fanout retains its narrow courtyard exception. This partial-copper screen does not close L4 coupling or measured crosstalk.
- The integrated board has **81 explicit moves, four replaced source copper items and 520 added copper items** against the functional-ECO baseline. Eight local T triplets and amplifier input/shunt connections are drawn on F.Cu; R444/C433 rotations avoid T/ground crossings, while K601/R417 moved 2.4 mm with rerouted copper and J701/J702 stayed fixed. The longest local T branches are 6.53/7.25/8.29 mm (Rin→Rout/capacitor→Rout/Rout→amplifier). Four I/V outputs now feed all eight first T resistors; the longest is **47.752 mm** on the right, and R411/R413 feeds are **27.289/41.648 mm**. The projected 2D I/V feedback model reaches **4.875 mm²**, excluding vertical and return-current area. DACL's U403→C417 feedback branch still lacks direct L2 for **1.9426 mm** of centreline, **0.0597 mm** more than the like-for-like prior baseline, so its return/stability extraction remains on HOLD. Central VREF copper reaches **14.422 mm**. Inspect C423/C426's 0.150–0.194 mm courtyard gaps and the C437/J701/K602 corridor in JLC DFM; extract the long cross-layer feeds.
- U501 local switching, VIN, cap and feedback groups have copper, with three short **0.20 mm** WSON VIN escapes. Its B.Cu `5V_ANA_F` feeder now detours around F.Cu `N5_FBP`, removing projected track and unshielded pad-inclusive overlap; the original power-via antipad remains. VIN3→C507 is **6.014 mm** against a 6.1 mm bound. C510/C511 GND vias are **8.160/6.767 mm** from the IC GND via by straight L2 via spacing; this is not a return-current extraction or a completed source-to-amplifier rail route.
- The owner approved a provisional ≤4.2 mm J701 TVS signal route with short L2 GND returns and a system IEC ESD test. The four J701 paths measure 3.83–4.03 mm; both J702 branches also meet the provisional screen. The functional-ECO baseline has an 8.311 mm hand-routed clock trunk and 1,144 full ratsnest gaps. The integrated study has **zero DRC violations, 499 KiCad-reported unconnected items and 973 full `pcbnew` ratsnest links**; G-3/G-4 and the other functional gates keep routing release on HOLD.
