# Schematic simplification review — 29 September 2026

## Conclusion

Only three high-confidence **review candidates** emerged from the current capture: the DNF MCLK monitor link R703, DNF debugger power link R241, and DNF shield capacitor C101. Each occupies one small PCB footprint while unpopulated. Removing one would also discard its stated measurement, debugging, or EMI option, so treat these as candidates for an owner decision and board-level review, not as approved deletions. Their combined gain is small and cannot resolve the main layout work.

Keep J701 (4.4 mm) and J702 (3.5 mm), and retain USB High power mode, per the owner's decisions. Retain the populated R417–R420 output links, output relays and TVS protection, protection comparators/timers, and amplifier hold-up parts unless a separate functional redesign is analyzed and validated.

The integrated board remains a routing study, not a release board.
